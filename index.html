Hi! I'm Cetrina, a marketing professional with experience in startup, academic, and non-profit environments.

My work can be defined by my History dissertation: focusing on South Korea's beauty standards, I analysed how cultural values continuously get reshaped within modern, globalised contexts to influence demand. What I took from it was the question (and skill) that applies to pretty much any brand: figuring out why an audience wants what it wants and how it got that way, then building an argument that actually holds up.

At GetHarley, I've used that lens across brand content, campaigns, and product marketing — producing the assets behind the sharpest growth stretch the Instagram channel has seen, and translating new AI product features into the emails and videos that introduced them to practitioners.

I'm currently open to roles in marketing, brand, PR, and client/customer relations. I love the mix of strategy and creativity, and I'm looking to keep growing with a team that works the same way.
